## Why actual external labels now?

After 0157 mixed-chemistry source-group MAE improvements, 0158 falsified whole-chemistry-family extrapolation, 0159 row screening showed objective-dependent pseudo-utility and 0160 showed most top-20% advantages disappear when candidates are counted **one connected source group per review unit**. The most informative no-cost next test is to measure the ability to predict values **from a different publication and independent primary data repository**, rather than further tune the repeatedly inspected OBELiX source TRAIN.

**Fixed training and models**: `NRC-Mila/OBELiX@4eaac889809dd489e3bae468c45d5c0e02b9147a`; 478 original official TRAIN, 29 original upper bounds excluded, 449 numeric TRAIN source rows and 102 DOI/composition-connected identity groups. Keep original official 121 TEST wholly quarantined. Fit `RandomForestRegressor` with original 0147 RF_PARAMS and elemental fractions (118 features), original TRAIN sample-group 0157 inverse-sqrt weighting or no weighting, plus original numeric TRAIN mean. **Geometry-assisted 0149 RF CANNOT be used on this external source because the original Caltech CSV has no licensed public CIF/unit-cell coordinates**; comparing composition-only RF is the valid common-input test. Freeze all models before parsing external values; never tune on external labels.

**Original external source**: [McHaffie et al. 571 experimental Li conductivity CSV on CaltechDATA](https://data.caltech.edu/records/23mvv-6gk43), exact original `ionic_conductivity_database.csv` MD5 `d1fcf7c9a7694c8f466932721b76b168` and SHA256 `868c4bd793d7c31b929787dc69478691b27ced0440d19269075143c98ecd1545`. Bound download size 36,122 bytes. The original CSV contains compound formula, ICSD *code* (not owned coordinates), original literature DOI, reported conductivity S/cm and `lowest_extrapolation_temperature_K`. The last field is **not** an attested exact measurement temperature. The corresponding paper is [McHaffie et al., Digital Discovery 2025](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5DD00052A). Reuse rights for raw CaltechDATA CSV remain unresolved, so its row labels, DOI strings, materials, ICSD codes and per-sample prediction scores are **never** committed or uploaded; retained Actions artifact has counts and aggregate metrics only.

3. Remove any further row whose normalized reduced composition matches an original OBELiX TRAIN formula. This still does **not** prove physical specimen or independence from original public TEST or model pretraining, but reduces obvious overlaps without touching original TEST.

## 0161 actual external primary-source numerical transfer results

Actual CaltechDATA first-party `ionic_conductivity_database.csv`: **571 published rows**, 565 externally parseable positive Li-bearing composition/experimental conductivity source-label rows, **6 rows not parseable as Li-bearing formula**, 225/571 records with an exact original **OBELiX TRAIN** DOI overlap signal (before parse filtering). 346/571 external rows with no exact TRAIN DOI signal initially, but **343** valid positive Li/formula sources after filtering. Strict no-DOI and no-reduced-formula signal cohort **301 rows / 280 reduced formula groups**, not 301 independently attested experimental specimens. The 121 original OBELiX public TEST sample fields and labels were never inspected.

**Negative generalization evidence:** unweighted RF performs better than balanced RF in all three external source-overlap tiers and formula-median evaluations. On the strictest cohort, the balanced RF is **0.09782923 log10 MAE worse** than unweighted RF (2.28821752 minus 2.19038829). Training-mean predictor is clearly worse than either RF in these observed public cross-corpus diagnostics, but all absolute errors are large and independent physical qualification is absent.

**Comparability limitations:** Original 0149/0157 geometry-assisted model from earlier experiments cannot be applied identically because Caltech CSV publishes an ICSD collection code but **not public source crystallographic coordinates or lattice parameters**; thus the 0161 0147 RF comparison uses **only common stoichiometry features**. Older 0157 source-group OOF geometry-assisted MAE 1.48588191 must NOT be compared as an identical experimental protocol with the strict external 2.19. Caltech samples derive from peer-reviewed literature and may indirectly overlap OBELiX public TEST, structure-based pretraining or source chemistry even after exact TRAIN DOI/formula exclusion. Reported source room-temperature conductivity and lowest extrapolation temperature are not original-paper sample-specific authenticated T or bulk-vs-total. A normalized composition group is not an independent experimentally measured specimen. The external raw CSV and per-row compounds, labels, DOI/ICSD identifiers never enter published artifacts.

**Milestone E status:** Actual numeric external source performance has been measured for the first time under a conservative original TRAIN-only zero-shot setup, but **no verified discovery advantage and no independently sealed blind study**. The low-cost baseline RF currently provides the better of two RF external diagnostics. Next 0162-E: data-independent replication with private/external custodian and explicitly attested material/measurement conditions, plus original MODEL-exposure check and frozen equal-budget physical candidate selection.
