## New physical source finding — two incompatible levels of truth

**Figure 6** is *one representative sample* with nominal Li1.6Al0.6Ge1.4(PO4)3 **sintered at 800°C**, measured by temperature-dependent impedance spectroscopy. Figure 6's Arrhenius curves use **inverse resistances** of bulk, grain-boundary and combined pathways rather than conductivity. A 25°C panel exists but does **not** imply the 800°C sintering temperature is the measurement temperature, nor that the neutron Rietveld CIF is derived from the same pellet. Inverse resistance has units Ω⁻¹; bulk and total conductivity in S/cm require valid electrode/sample geometry and measurement methods, and unknown grain-boundary thickness prevents naive grain-boundary conductivity calculation.

**Figure 7** is **not a single-pellet chart**. The original authors explicitly report *room-temperature bulk and total ionic conductivity* versus **nominal composition**, and state that plotted values are **averages over multiple samples with identical nominal composition**, with **uncertainty as standard deviation**. The actual physical measurement unit is therefore a `NOMINAL_COMPOSITION_PELLET_POPULATION`, not the original source CIF single-sample transport label. The original paper's x≈0.5 solubility limit, loss of main LAGP phase and secondary phases at high x cause substantial microstructural and actual-composition confounding. A nominal x=.6 physical specimen cannot be modeled as a guaranteed single-phase x=.6 or as automatically equivalent to the diffraction-refined x=.480.

Both original source-processed TRAIN labels still have arithmetic log10 difference **+0.76937121** (0162/0163). But the original processed CSV does not identify which physical pellet, bulk vs total source point, uncertainty, temperature and measured phase produced either processed value, nor if it corresponds to an original figure population mean. The separate original raw censoring policy still excludes 29 of 478 point-looking processed records for actual usable regression. No label promotion.
