## Falsifiable original-science question: is stoichiometry alone sufficient to predict measured conductivity?

A deterministic predictor `f(nominal_stoichiometry)` returns one constant across all **six original processing conditions** (same nominal chemistry). In log10 σ(S/cm) loss, the empirically optimal constant is their median `log10(σ)`; the minimum possible mean absolute deviation on these six PUBLIC labels is **0.37447227 log10**, with **max/min sigma 10.21052632×** and **range 1.00904813 log10 decades**. The source trend is non-monotone: 500°C processing produces lower reported conductivity than 400°C, yet 550°C is highest. The minimum source Ea is at 500°C, but maximum σ is at 550°C. These are genuine experimental conditioning counterexamples; **not** a causal estimate of processing temperature, not unseen-case ML performance, not a mathematically global generalization floor, and not author-attested independent pellets. The six processed records may differ in phase fraction/transport paths; without exact prepared specimen/phase correspondence they cannot directly verify MD.
