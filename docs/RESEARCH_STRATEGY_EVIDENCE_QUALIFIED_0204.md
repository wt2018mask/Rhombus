# Rhombus 0204 — Owner-approved research priority and success contract

Date: 2026-10-30. Decision status: **OWNER APPROVED — STRATEGIC PRIORITY FROZEN**. Implementation and scientific qualification remain separate. This decision supersedes prior **forward-looking priority orders**, not historical scientific data, immutable records, numerical definitions, or the required digital-qualification mission.

## Identity and outcome

**Product identity:** Rhombus is a domain-neutral, AI-native, evidence-qualified scientific research control plane. Solid-state lithium ionic conductors are its first scientific application. Its operational end product is defensible **digital scientific qualification and material/candidate selection under a bounded, comparable compute budget**. It is not required to discover an experimentally verified new material, replace all MLIPs or DFT, or conduct new lab measurements.

**First falsifiable scientific claim to attempt:** On a *single defined Li migration-hop barrier prediction task*, Rhombus's evidence and numerical diagnostics, explicit abstention and high-fidelity escalation must improve actionable decision quality against a strong frozen single-model baseline **at matched compute/observation budget**. Specifically demonstrate either lower false-acceptance risk at the same coverage, or higher coverage at the same error-risk ceiling, with actual cost and uncertainty reported. All-abstain is not a success.

Model-intrinsic energy/force MAE, numerical edge correctness, single-hop NEB barriers, periodic 3D percolation thresholds, dynamic diffusion and experimental conductivity are **different observables**. Do not equate them, use incompatible reference energies or combine their scores to imply physics qualification.

## Binding priority order

1. **P0 — Freeze the evaluation question and admissible evidence.** Locate public, provenance-verified Li migration-hop DFT-NEB reference data; verify observable and structures, magnetic/charge/electronic settings when available, license and material/DOI-group disjointness. Audit prior MLIP training exposure and disclose unverifiable leakage. Freeze an independent held-out group, baseline model(s), cheap/no-information baselines, evaluation budget, admissible decisions and thresholds *before* scoring. If evidence is inadequate, record NO_DATA / ABSTAIN rather than manufacture a benchmark.
2. **P1 — Leverage existing numerical falsification assets.** Reproduce the exact Priority 4 26-neighbor diagonal-edge hidden-barrier counterexample. Timebox the G0–G1 certified-segment diagnostic to **10 working days**. Only claim a rigorous segment maximum bracket when the necessary function bounds, periodic images, cutoff and floating-point assumptions are actually justified. Without a bound, label DIAGNOSTIC or ABSTAIN. This is a *supporting capability*, not a mandatory global certification.
3. **P2 — Evaluate decision improvements.** On sealed, source-/composition-group-separated evaluation units, measure empirical false-acceptance risk, coverage, error versus reference, score/calibration or risk–coverage curves, and wall/CPU/memory/model-evaluation cost. Perform matched-budget paired comparisons and group-aware uncertainty; disclose sample size, confounding, training overlap and abstained cases. No test-set threshold tuning. Distinguish retrospective public benchmark evidence from prospective blind generalization.
4. **P3 — Integrate the smallest useful MCP capability** only after evidence supports it; provenance- and applicability-gated accept/abstain/escalate status and exact reasons, with native `rhombus` API naming and unchanged historical claims. Avoid new server services, duplicate scheduling or wide APIs.
5. **P4 — Optional later research:** certified global 3D percolation, alternate chemical families, collective-ion dynamics, actual conductivity and new Hamiltonians. Promote only with a fresh falsifiable hypothesis, independent scientific basis, cost justification and owner authorization.

## Advancement gates and stopping rules

- Gate 0: Frozen reference/observable, leakage-aware split, strong comparable baseline, compute budget and decision loss. If not met, **stop performance comparisons** and collect data; do not substitute a different observable.
- Gate 1: Reproduce adversarial edge counterexample; bound only verified subdomains. Stop the G0–G1 timebox if computational rigor or applicability is missing; retain negative results.
- Gate 2: Pre-registered equal-budget baseline comparison on untouched groups. Must show a scientifically meaningful paired advantage or conclude NOT_DEMONSTRATED. Selective prediction must report both **coverage and error**, not just abstention.
- Gate 3: Only after Gate 2, integrate a *restricted* MCP flow that emits uncertainty, missing-evidence and applicability codes and NEVER invents qualification.
- Each unsuccessful gate retains the last proven capability; no automatic expansion.

## Success metrics / reference procedure

The preregistered protocol must state: target error metric (e.g. MAE in eV), error-severity threshold for false acceptance, accepted fraction (coverage), false-acceptance confidence interval, source-/material-group bootstrap or equivalent, model evaluation count, extra numerical diagnostic cost, and actual decision-value utility. A threshold that was not frozen before held-out evaluation is exploratory, not confirmatory.

A trivial abstain-all policy has risk undefined/zero with **zero coverage** and never wins by default. An unfiltered high-coverage predictor and a cheap no-information/heuristic predictor are included to reveal apparent gains due only to filtering or cost asymmetry.
