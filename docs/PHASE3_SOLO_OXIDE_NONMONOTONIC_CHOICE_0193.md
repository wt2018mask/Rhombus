# 0193 — Nonmonotonic manufacturing choice: no free win from always hotter

Checkpoint 0192 exposed a critical confound: a learned process correction chose all five hotter (1100°C) 2022 ACS pairs correctly, but so did a zero-training always-hotter heuristic. Moreover, the source measured at unspecified room temperature, not authenticated exactly 25°C. That result cannot be counted as a new strict exact-25°C selection win.

This executable source-admissibility gate compares previously authenticated 2018 RSC, 2020 Materials, and 2022 ACS evidence without retrieving or claiming a new paper. Original 2018 LLZALO-4 is optimal at 1210°C although 1220°C LLZALO-5 is available, so always-hotter fails. The 0191 process model also selected LLZALO-5 and failed. In the 2020 study the observed best SL2 occurs at a tied maximum 1200°C; naive hottest-only tie breaking must abstain rather than claim a correct pick. The 2022 ACS study is excluded from strict 25°C because the authors only specify room temperature.

**Important boundary:** The 2018 nonmonotonic example was already used in 0191. It is a negative-control case, NOT a new blind benchmark. The present checkpoint adds **zero new source DOI** and **zero newly evaluated labels**. Its purpose is to prevent the 0192 heuristic-tie from becoming an inflated selection-success claim. A proper new original-paper-out test requires at least one new exact-25°C original source with measured nonmonotonic sintering response, comparable furnace descriptors, and pre-specified source embargo. Until then verdict INCONCLUSIVE, production OFF, physical P0 0/3 BLOCKED.
